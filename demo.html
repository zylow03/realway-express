What the AI decides

Why it matters

1

Bad parcels stay in China

Every parcel is checked against its declaration before loading. One illegal item could get the whole container seized at a border.

2

Zones in drop-off order

Laos sits by the door and Singapore at the back, so each stop unloads only its own zone. Turnaround at each station stays short.

3

A sealed zone per country

Separate seals and papers for each zone mean goods pass through Laos and Thailand in transit, without paying transit taxes.

4

Spare space is revenue

The map shows exactly how many metres are free, so leftover space can be sold to the next pool of sellers before departure.
