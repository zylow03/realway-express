The package bus

How it works

One container, many sellers, four countries. Each parcel rides in the zone for its country, so the train can unload at every stop without unpacking the rest.

Step 1

Drop at the hub

Send cartons to our Kunming or Chongqing hub before the cut-off. Print our label at home.

Step 2

Screened and sorted

Every parcel is X-rayed and checked against its declaration. Our AI plans the wagon load.

Step 3

Loaded in drop order

Laos goes by the door, then Thailand, Malaysia, and Singapore at the back. Each zone has its own customs papers.

Step 4

Dropped and delivered

At each stop the zone comes off and a local courier such as J&T or Ninja Van takes it to the door.

    One bad box can stop a whole container

    What we can't carry

    Containers cross four borders. If one parcel breaks the rules, customs can hold everyone's goods. Every parcel is X-rayed in China, and anything on this list is returned to the sender.

    Loose lithium batteriesPower banks, spare cells, e-bike packs
    Liquids & aerosolsPerfume, sprays, paint, e-liquid
    Food & supplementsSnacks, tea, vitamins, pet food
    CounterfeitsCopies of branded goods or logos
    Medicines & drugsPrescription or over-the-counter
    Weapons & replicasKnives, airsoft, parts, toy guns
    FlammablesLighters, matches, fuel, solvents
    Cash & valuablesCurrency, gold, jewellery

    Allowed with extra paperwork

    Electronics with built-in batteriesBattery spec sheet (MSDS) needed
    CosmeticsIngredient list, sealed packaging
    Textiles & apparelHS code and fabric content
    Children's toysSafety certificate for TH and MY

    Questions

    FAQ

    Who pays import duty and tax?

    By default the buyer pays duty and GST or VAT on delivery (DDU). Sellers can choose to prepay it (DDP) when booking, so the buyer pays nothing extra at the door.

    Will my parcel be charged transit tax in Laos or Thailand?

    No. Each country's goods travel in their own sealed zone under transit papers, so only the destination country's customs clears your parcel.

    What happens if the train is delayed?

    Tracking updates at every station. If a delay passes 48 hours, we email or WhatsApp you and the buyer with a new date.

    How do I claim for a damaged parcel?

    Send photos within 7 days of delivery from your Seller hub. Insured parcels are paid up to the declared value. Uninsured parcels are covered up to US$5 per kg.

    Who delivers to the buyer's door?

    Local partners in each country, for example J&T Express or Ninja Van in Malaysia. The tracking number stays the same.
