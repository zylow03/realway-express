Engineering validation required

The modular container system is an engineering concept. It must be assessed with railway operators, terminal operators and container-equipment specialists before it could operate. Four nominal 10 ft modules do not prove the arrangement fits an existing 40 ft wagon or a certified handling system.

  • Wagon compatibility and available deck length
  • Loading-gauge restrictions on each railway
  • Individual module dimensions and corner-fitting positions
  • Combined weight and axle loads
  • Securing method between module and wagon
  • Lifting equipment and spreader compatibility
  • Terminal handling capability and dwell time at each stop
  • Cross-border customs and regulatory approvals

No certification status, equipment supplier, load rating or handling time is claimed in this prototype.
