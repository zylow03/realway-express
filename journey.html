Real-world validation required before operation
Railway service
  • Carrier quotations and written confirmation of China–Thailand–Malaysia arrangements
  • Origin & destination terminals, frequency, transit times
  • Gauge-transfer arrangements, terminal charges, liability & insurance
Laos transfer operations
  • Direct rail-to-rail or road transfer
  • Handling terminal and onward metre-gauge capacity
  • Transfer & storage charges; customs transit
Thailand & Malaysia rail
  • Actual service connections and handovers
  • Padang Besar procedures; service availability to PIP
  • Terminal booking and receiving requirements
Customs
  • Chinese export, Lao and Thai transit, Malaysian import
  • Classification, permits, duties, bonds or guarantees
  • Rules for consolidated cargo and inspections
Cargo cages
  • Dimensions, tare weight, rated payload, strength
  • Forklift compatibility, foldability, maintenance
  • Purchase, return and repositioning cost
  • The 16-cage layout is not an approved loading plan
Phase 1Standard shared-container service with pallets, existing partners and the RealWay platform. Prove SME demand and coordination economics.
Phase 2Small cage pilot in a controlled warehouse. Measure loading time, protection, traceability, utilisation and return cost against pallets.
Phase 3Detachable 10 ft module concept stays long-term R&D. Launch does not depend on custom railway hardware.
