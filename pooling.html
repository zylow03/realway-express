When a pool doesn't fill

Pool merging

Two compatible booking batches are each only partly full. Sending both as separate full-container departures pays the fixed cost twice. Merging them fills one container and releases the other.

The mechanism in one view

How Kongsi Pooling works

Many customers ship smallShoppers, resellers and merchants each send a few parcels.
We consolidate compatible demandParcels with matching destinations, timing and cargo rules go into one pool.
Customers share freight capacityEach pays for their parcels, not for a whole container.
We coordinate logistics partnersWarehouse, freight, border transfer, customs and last-mile partners are paid per departure.
The platform tracks viabilityUtilisation and contribution are calculated live, so each departure decision is clear.
Better pooling, better economicsLess unused space spreads the fixed cost across more parcels and improves contribution.
