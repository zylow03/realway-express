Multi-Stop Kongsi · China ⇄ Laos ⇄ Thailand ⇄ Malaysia

One container, many customers, many borders

RealWay is not only for importing from China into Malaysia. It is proposed as a pooling network between participating hubs: an SME in any of the four countries books cage space from its own hub to any later hub on the line. This page shows how that could work, what already exists, and what still has to be proven.

Existing technologyOpen-top containers, hardtop containers, cranes and cargo restraints. In use today.
Established logistics practiceAuthorised container handling, customs transit and warehouse consolidation. Done daily, under rules.
Proposed RealWay innovationRepeated cage exchanges at intermediate hubs and resale of freed capacity along a cross-border rail corridor. Not yet proven.

    3 · Cargo dividers

    Restraint is engineered, not a loose plate

    Between positions RealWay proposes a removable blocking frame. It only stops cargo moving if it is designed for the actual cage masses and train forces, installed correctly and locked to the container’s structure.

    • ✓ Rated frame, designed for the real load and train accelerations
    • ✓ Locked to the container structure with pins (green = locked)
    • ✓ Hold-down bar over each cage
    • ✓ Inspected and signed off after every exchange
    • ✗ A loose divider plate is not sufficient restraint
    • ✗ Gaps let cages build up speed when the train brakes
    • ✗ Never move the container while any frame is out
    • ! Whenever a cage is removed or replaced, the remaining cargo must be re-secured

    6 · What must be proven

    Open questions before any pilot

    CustomsWhether Lao and Thai customs will authorise partial unloading and loading of a container in transit, and under which procedure (for example national transit rules or the ASEAN Customs Transit System).
    Hub capabilityWhich hubs have the crane, hardstanding, staging space, customs presence and time window. Not every station can do this.
    EngineeringCage design, rated lifting lugs and frame, blocking-frame strength, container lashing-point capacity and certification for rail forces.
    Railway approvalRail operators in each country must accept the load securing method and the extra dwell time at hubs.
    Terminal timeHow long one exchange takes, and whether it fits the train schedule without delaying other customers.
    Liability & insuranceWho is responsible for each cage at each handover, and how claims work across four countries.

    Open-top (22U/U1-type) and hardtop containers are standard ISO equipment. Every other element here is a proposal for investigation.
